Privacy Policy for Water Reminder

Last updated: October 2026

Water Reminder provides a hydration tracking service designed to help users log their daily water intake.

1. Information Collection and Use

  • Location Data: The app requests location permissions solely to determine local weather conditions and adjust hydration suggestions. This location data is processed locally and is not sold or shared with data brokers.
  • Local Storage: Daily water intake logs and reminder preferences remain stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device:

  • Google AdMob: We display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, and diagnostic crash data to serve advertisements and detect invalid traffic.

Review Google's Privacy & Terms: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
